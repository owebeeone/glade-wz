### [SAF-P2-12] R9's new "Durable cost on the first boot" column counts only the spelling churn and omits the R2-forced `windowed` edits, so b1's and b2's stated costs (0 and 13) are wrong under the recommended R2(a) — and §4.7 row 9 turns those numbers into a gate assertion

**Root cause.** The durable-cost column attributes appends only to R9's own spelling mechanism. Two of the 28 binding lines change because of **R2's** answer, not R9's, and those two change the same `toks[5]` through the same code path. Row (a) of the table accounts for them; rows (b1) and (b2) do not.

**Location.** §3 R9's option table: row **(a)** at `:871`, row **(b1)** at `:872`, row **(b2)** at `:873`. §4.4 bullet 6 at `:1621-1626`. §4.7(b) row 9 at `:1836`. Supporting: §3 R2's option table rows (a) at `:590` and (b) at `:591`.

**Violated invariant.** Internal consistency between two cells of the same table, and the property §4.7 row 9 is written to enforce: "**The asserted `appended` is the chosen option's own stated durable cost and must equal it**".

**Reproduction (arithmetic, over a path I verified end to end).** Row **(a)**'s cost cell reads, verbatim:

> **15 appends** if the file side respells (13 `from-cursor` + 2 `windowed`), **2** if it does not — the `windowed` lines change under every R2 option, so they are the floor

So the document itself states that when the file side does **not** respell, the durable cost is **2** — the `windowed` lines. Now row **(b1)** is precisely "the file side does not respell": its App-files cell says "**0 edits for the 13 `from-cursor` lines**; **2 edits for `windowed`**, which is a meaning change, not a spelling one". Its durable-cost cell nevertheless reads "**0.** No stored byte changes."

Those two cells cannot both be true. Trace the two `windowed` lines through the path the document itself establishes: an edit to token 5 changes `toks[5]`; `appdecl.rs:138` writes it into `sysdata::BindingDecl.retention` (`:37` imports that type from `crate::sysdata`); `sysdata.rs:135` encodes it at tag 6 as `Cbor::Text`; `registry.rs:97` dispatches `Record::Binding` to that `to_cbor`; `appdecl.rs:264` diffs `(glade_id, payload)` and no stored record matches; `:267` appends and `glade-node.rs:91` persists. **Two appends.** The same reasoning row (a) applies, applied to row (b1).

The same omission propagates to **(b2)**, whose App-files cell is "as (b1)". b2's 13 normalised records plus the 2 `windowed` file edits are **15** appends, not the stated 13.

§4.4 bullet 6 states the contradiction in the plainest available form at `:1621-1626`: "Under R9(b1) or (b2): **0 edits for the 13 `from-cursor` lines**, 2 for `windowed`. … The **durable** cost of each is R9's 'Durable cost on the first boot' column, and it is not the same number: 0 under b1, 13 under b2, 15 under (a)/(c)." Two file edits and a durable cost of zero, in consecutive sentences.

A second, smaller error sits inside row (a)'s otherwise-correct cell: "the `windowed` lines change under every R2 option, so they are the floor" is false under **R2(b)**, whose own App-files cell at `:591` reads "0 meaning edits" because (b) keeps `windowed` as an enum member. So the floor is 2 under R2(a)/(c)/(d) and 0 under R2(b). Both errors point the same way: **0 and 13 are the numbers only under R2(b), which is not the recommendation.** Under the document's recommended set — R2(a) with R9(b) — the true numbers are **2** under b1 and **15** under b2. I grepped every occurrence of "windowed" and of "floor" in the object; nothing anywhere reconciles the two cells.

**Impact.** Three consequences, in ascending order.

1. *The owner is given a false trade-off.* R9's recommendation at `:912-913` asks the owner to choose "within (b), b1 if the durable cost is what matters and b2 if a single stored vocabulary is". The stated spread is 0 against 13. The real spread under the recommended R2(a) is 2 against 15. The choice is still defensible on the same grounds, but "b1 costs nothing durable" is the sentence doing the persuading, and it is not true.
2. *The closure gate is specified to assert a number the recommended answers falsify.* §4.7(b) row 9 requires the asserted `appended` to equal the stated cost: "**0** under (b1), **13** under (b2)". Written that way, the test is red on first run under R2(a) — and it is the regression test for the R9 lineage, the one artefact meant to prove the durable story is understood. A red gate at a one-shot migration is where an executor starts adjusting the assertion to match observed behaviour, which is precisely the inversion these gates exist to prevent.
3. *§4.4 bullet 5's page obligation is mis-scoped for b1.* `:1601-1610` says the page owes the fold and retract rules for fifteen surfaces under R9(b), then explains "Under **b1** no record moves". Two records do move, so the two `windowed` surfaces need the same "did my migration take effect?" answer under b1 that the 13 need under b2 — and they are the one dual-maintained surface, the most visible in the tree.

This is bounded and loud: the wrong number surfaces the first time the row-9 test runs, and nothing silently corrupts. That is why it is P2 and not P1.
